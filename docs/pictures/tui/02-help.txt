 › /help
Type a question for KeelBot, or a command:

Flows
  /project [name]                      pick the project (or switch to one by name)
  /flow [workflow] [idea…]             start a flow: pick the workflow, then say the idea
  /flows                               this project's flows, newest first
  /resume                              go on with a stopped or failed flow from its last saved step
  /stop                                stop the project's flow (every finished step stays saved)
  /status                              the flow: step, phase, criteria, what waits, tokens
  /next                                what to do next
  /workflows [workflow]                the project's workflows, and a workflow as a diagram

Gates
  /inbox                               everything that waits for you, in every project
  /approve [n]                         approve what waits (or inbox item n)
  /reject [n] <why>                    send back what waits, with the reason
  /answer [choice | q1=answer …]       answer a gate's questions or choose one of its exits

Look
  /jobs                                the project's agent calls, newest first
  /live                                the agents at work now and their last step
  /steps [job]                         the flow's steps with their state (or one agent call's steps)
  /timeline                            what happened: steps and agent runs, newest first
  /diagram <flow|workflow <id>|map-er [table]|map-system|map-modules|graph <symbol>> [zoom]
                                       keel's diagrams in ASCII
  /rules                               keel's rules for this project, and what agents may change
right now
  /plugins                             the plugins keel has, and which are on here
  /budget                              tokens and money today, this month, per flow, and the caps
  /dashboard [flow|map|er|keelbot|inbox]
                                       the dashboard's link for this project

KeelBot
  /model                               KeelBot's model (from keel's catalog)
  /chats                               earlier KeelBot chats of this project: open one
  /new                                 a new KeelBot chat
  /mode <ask|fix>                      KeelBot's mode: ask (reads, changes nothing) or fix (keel's
rules apply)

Screen
  /help                                what keel TUI can do
  /panel <flow|agents|inbox|off>       show a panel, or none (keys: Ctrl+F, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+B toggle
them)
  /clear                               clear the screen
  /quit                                leave keel TUI (keel keeps running)

Keys
  Tab complete the command or its argument · Up / Down the history, or the completion to take ·
Ctrl+F toggle the flow rail · Ctrl+A toggle the agents at work · Ctrl+B toggle the inbox · Esc stop
KeelBot's answer, or cancel a choice · Ctrl+C twice leave (keel keeps running)

KeelBot's own commands (/gate, /where …) go to KeelBot as they are.
